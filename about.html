About the Foundation

An independent nonprofit advancing data-driven public good.

Who we are

StreetPulse Foundation ("Street Pulse Foundation Inc.") is a nonprofit nonstock corporation organized in the State of Delaware in 2026. Our purpose is exclusively charitable, educational, and scientific: we support programs that generate public knowledge, build human capacity, and improve safety, resilience, and equity in cities — and in the oceans and coastal regions connected to them.

How we work

  • Research in the public interest — rigorous, transparent, and published openly wherever possible.
  • Open data and tools — maps, dashboards, and datasets made freely available to researchers, journalists, and policymakers.
  • Education and workforce development — fellowships, internships, and training in AI, geospatial data, and urban analytics.
  • Partnership — working with communities, academic institutions, and public agencies.

Governance and status

The Foundation is governed by an independent board of directors and operates under adopted governance policies covering conflicts of interest, financial controls, whistleblower protections, and document retention. We are organized as a Delaware nonprofit corporation; federal recognition of 501(c)(3) tax-exempt status is in progress. The Foundation maintains complete legal, financial, and operational separation from any for-profit entities.
