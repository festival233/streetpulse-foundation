StreetPulse Foundation supports charitable, scientific, and educational programs that turn street-level and environmental data into public good — from road safety and climate intelligence to open research and the blue economy.

Explore our programs Contact us

Our mission

We exist to make cities safer, more resilient, and more equitable — and to extend that work to the oceans and coasts that sustain them. We pursue this through rigorous research, open data, education, and partnerships with communities, researchers, and public institutions.

Program areas

Road Safety & Infrastructure Intelligence

Research and public-interest data on road conditions, traffic safety, and infrastructure quality — especially in under-mapped cities.

Climate & Environmental Intelligence

Scientific programs on urban climate risk, air quality, flooding, and environmental monitoring for public benefit.

AI, GIS & Data Education

Fellowships, internships, and workforce development that train the next generation in geospatial data, AI, and urban analytics.

Public-Interest Research & Open Data

Open maps, dashboards, and datasets published for researchers, journalists, and policymakers — free for public use.

StreetPulse Blue

Blue-economy, ocean, and coastal programs: research and education for healthy oceans and resilient coastal communities.
