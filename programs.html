Programs

Five program areas, one goal: data in service of the public good.

Road Safety & Infrastructure Intelligence

Charitable and scientific programs producing public-interest intelligence on road conditions, traffic safety, and infrastructure quality — with a focus on under-mapped cities where reliable data is scarcest and the need is greatest.

Climate & Environmental Intelligence

Research and monitoring programs on urban climate risk — including flooding, heat, and air quality — that give communities and public institutions the evidence they need to adapt and protect public health.

AI, GIS & Data Education

Fellowships, internships, and workforce-development programs that train students and early-career professionals in artificial intelligence, geographic information systems, and urban data analytics.

Public-Interest Research & Open Data

Open maps, dashboards, datasets, and published research — freely available to researchers, journalists, educators, and policymakers, and designed for reuse in the public interest.

StreetPulse Blue

Our blue-economy, ocean, and coastal portfolio: scientific and educational programs supporting healthy oceans, sustainable coastal livelihoods, and resilient coastal communities.

Partner with us. We welcome conversations with researchers, educators, funders, and public institutions whose missions align with ours.
