ABOUT

Local intelligence.
Public purpose.

StreetPulse Foundation is being built as a public-interest platform for environmental, geospatial and community-centered intelligence.

MISSION

Make locally relevant intelligence more useful to the places that need it.

Our mission is to advance public-interest environmental and geospatial intelligence that helps communities and institutions understand changing conditions, strengthen resilience and make better decisions.

VISION

A future where geography does not determine access to useful local intelligence.

We envision communities across Africa and emerging markets having stronger access to the observations, tools, research and partnerships needed to plan, adapt and prosper.
