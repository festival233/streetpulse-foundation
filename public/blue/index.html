01 — WHY BLUE

The coast is an economy,
an ecosystem and a frontline.

Coastal communities can face overlapping pressures from flooding, erosion, pollution, infrastructure stress, changing livelihoods and fragmented environmental data. The same coastlines also hold enormous economic and ecological opportunity.

PRESSURESFlooding + erosionPollution + runoffChanging fisheriesInfrastructure stressClimate risk
OPPORTUNITYFisheries + aquaculturePorts + logisticsTourismMarine technologyBlue finance

StreetPulse Blue's initial geographic direction centers on Ghana, with a regional opportunity across the Gulf of Guinea and West Africa.

03 — PILOT FRAMEWORK

From pilot to coastal
intelligence infrastructure.

A staged model designed to start focused, learn quickly and build the evidence needed for responsible scale.

PHASE 01Baseline

Map conditions, partners and available data.

PHASE 02Observe

Integrate appropriate environmental, geospatial and community data sources.

PHASE 03Understand

Analyze hotspots, trends, infrastructure exposure and economic implications.

PHASE 04Share & Scale

Create useful outputs for communities, researchers and decision-makers.

This is a program model, not a record of completed work.
