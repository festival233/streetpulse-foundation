01 — WHY DATA

You cannot strengthen
what you cannot see.

Rapidly growing communities can face serious information gaps around infrastructure, flooding, air quality, mobility, coastal change and environmental conditions.

01

Local visibility

Build a clearer picture of conditions at the street, neighborhood and coastal level.

02

Decision intelligence

Translate observations and data into patterns that institutions and communities can actually use.

03

Public value

Support research, planning and resilience through responsible access to locally relevant information.

04

Shared capacity

Strengthen the people, partnerships and tools needed to sustain local intelligence over time.

FLAGSHIP PROGRAM

STREETPULSEBLUE

Intelligence for coastlines.
Opportunity for communities.

StreetPulse Blue is the Foundation's flagship Blue Economy initiative, designed to combine environmental intelligence, geospatial data, community knowledge and technology to support healthier coastlines, more resilient livelihoods and better-informed coastal development.
