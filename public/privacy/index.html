PRIVACY POLICY

Plain-language privacy.

This website is an informational site. We collect as little as possible, and this page explains what that means in practice.

Last updated: October 2026.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Almost nothing directly.

This site has no accounts, no forms that store data on our servers, and no newsletter signup that records your details. If you email us, we receive whatever you choose to include in that email so we can respond.

ANALYTICS

Aggregate, privacy-friendly measurement.

We may use privacy-friendly web analytics to understand overall visits to this site — for example, which pages are viewed. This measurement does not use advertising cookies and is not used to build advertising profiles of visitors.

TECHNICAL LOGS

Standard web operations.

Like nearly every website, our hosting and security provider processes standard technical information needed to deliver the site safely — such as IP addresses and request details — for security, reliability and abuse prevention. We do not sell this information.

COOKIES

No advertising cookies.

This site does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Strictly necessary technical storage may be used by our hosting provider to deliver the site securely.

YOUR INFORMATION

Sharing, retention and your rights.

We do not sell personal information. If you contact us by email, we keep that correspondence only as long as needed to respond and for ordinary organizational records. If you would like us to delete correspondence you sent us, email info@streetpulsefoundation.org and we will do so where we reasonably can.

CHANGES & CONTACT

Updates to this policy.

We may update this page as the site and the organization develop; the "last updated" date above will reflect the current version. Questions about this policy: info@streetpulsefoundation.org.
